Editorial

The Gazette's Chief Editor Speaks on the Art of the Catalogue

This publication was conceived in the finest tradition of journalistic enterprise — a desire to bring order, clarity, and typographic elegance to the chaotic world of motion picture records. What you see before you is not merely a film catalogue, but a testament to craft.

"Every film deserves to be remembered. Every record deserves to be beautiful."

Built as a practical exercise in the Angular framework, this project explores components, routing, signals, and the ancient art of making things look splendid. The author accepts full responsibility for any excess of gothic typography.

— ✦ —

The Film Gazette is a student project created as part of RS School Angular Course 2026. All film data is mock. Any resemblance to actual journalism is purely ornamental.
